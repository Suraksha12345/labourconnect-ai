# Karnataka Minimum Wages - Structure & Rules

Source: Karnataka Minimum Wages Act, 1958 / Central Minimum Wages Act, 1948, and state notifications

## Zone Classification
Karnataka divides the state into wage zones based on economic development level:
- **Zone 1** — Greater Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) range
- **Zone 2** — All other Municipal Corporations and identified agglomerated urban areas
- **Zone 3 / Zone 4** — Remaining regions of the state (including most rural areas)

Wages are generally highest in Zone 1 and lowest in Zone 3/4, reflecting cost-of-living differences.

## Skill Categories
Workers are classified into four skill levels for wage-fixing purposes:
- **Highly Skilled** — high proficiency, capable of supervising others and working independently (e.g. site supervisors, skilled technicians)
- **Skilled** — trained and can work independently in their trade (e.g. masons, electricians, carpenters)
- **Semi-Skilled** — basic operational skill, works under supervision (e.g. mason's helper, farm labour)
- **Unskilled** — manual labour requiring no specific trade skill (e.g. loading/unloading, general helper)

## Key Wage Protection Rules
- Wages must be paid directly into the worker's bank account (Payment of Wages Act, Section 6)
- Part-time workers must be paid proportionate to a full 8-hour day; if they work less than 8 hours, wages are calculated on a daily average basis
- Overtime (work beyond the normal daily hours) must be paid at double the normal wage rate
- Equal wages must be paid to men and women for the same or similar work
- Basic wages and Dearness Allowance (DA) amounts are rounded to the nearest rupee
- Contract labour engaged under the Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Act must also receive at least the applicable minimum wage

## Dearness Allowance (VDA)
Karnataka revises the Variable Dearness Allowance periodically (often twice a year) to account for inflation, added on top of the base minimum wage. Rates are published in the official gazette notification for each period.

## Important Note for This App
Exact rupee figures (daily/monthly minimum wage per zone and skill category) change with each gazette notification, typically twice yearly. **Do not hardcode specific rupee amounts into the Wage Advisor agent's permanent knowledge** — instead, treat this document as the rule structure, and update the actual rate figures whenever a new notification is issued by the Karnataka Labour Department. Check the latest notification at the Karnataka Department of Labour's official site before publishing exact numbers to users.
