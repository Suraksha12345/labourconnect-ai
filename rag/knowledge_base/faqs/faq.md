# LabourConnect - Frequently Asked Questions

## Account & Login
**Q: How do I log in to LabourConnect?**
A: LabourConnect uses OTP-based login. Enter your registered mobile number, and you'll receive a one-time password via SMS to verify and log in. Once logged in, your session stays active so you don't need to log in every time.

**Q: Can I use the app in my own language?**
A: Yes. LabourConnect supports Kannada, Hindi, English, and Tulu. You can change your language preference in the app settings, and it will be remembered for future sessions.

## Finding Work
**Q: How does job matching work?**
A: You set your skills (e.g. masonry, farm labour, electrical work) in your profile. The app matches you with nearby job postings that need those skills, using your location and the contractor's requirements.

**Q: How do I know the wage offered is fair?**
A: The Wage Advisor assistant can tell you the applicable minimum wage for your skill category and zone in Karnataka, so you can check any job offer against the legal minimum before accepting.

**Q: What if a contractor doesn't pay me as agreed?**
A: Use the "Report Suspicious Job" feature to flag the contractor. This creates a record that can support a labour department complaint. For wage disputes, registered BOCW workers can also seek help through the Labour Inspector.

## Safety
**Q: How do I know if a job is safe?**
A: The Safety Check assistant reviews job details and flags known risks for that type of work (e.g. working at height, electrical work, chemical exposure) and gives you basic safety precautions before you accept.

**Q: What do I do if I'm injured at work?**
A: Seek first aid immediately, inform your contractor/site supervisor, and if you're a registered construction worker, you may be eligible for accident compensation through KBOCWWB — ask the chatbot for the claims process.

## Registration & Benefits
**Q: Am I eligible for government welfare schemes?**
A: If you're a registered construction worker with KBOCWWB (or registered on e-Shram for other unorganised work), you may be eligible for medical, education, pension, and accident benefits. Ask the chatbot "am I eligible for [scheme name]" for specifics.

**Q: I don't have proof of 90 days of work — can I still register?**
A: Yes, an employer or union/NGO certification can often substitute for formal proof. Ask the chatbot for the current alternative documentation accepted.

## Privacy & Data
**Q: Is my personal data safe on LabourConnect?**
A: Your data is stored securely via Firebase and only used to match you with jobs and provide relevant assistance. It is not shared with third parties beyond what's needed to connect you with contractors.
