# Construction & Daily Labour Site Safety Guidelines

Source: Derived from BOCW Act/Rules safety provisions (Sections 28-39) plus standard construction safety practice.

## Mandatory Site Facilities (Legal Requirement)
Every construction site employing workers must provide:
- Clean drinking water within reasonable distance of the work area
- Separate latrines and urinals for men and women workers
- Crèche facilities if a minimum number of women workers with young children are employed
- A first-aid box, stocked and accessible, at every worksite
- A canteen at larger sites employing above a threshold number of workers

## Equipment & Machinery Safety
- All lifting appliances (cranes, hoists, winches) must be tested and certified before use, with certificates renewed periodically
- Only qualified, certified operators may run lifting equipment; a trained signaller must assist
- Moving machinery parts (motors, gears) must be properly fenced to prevent accidental contact
- Passageways and stairs must be adequately illuminated at all times workers are present

## Hazard Controls
- Excessive noise and vibration exposure must be controlled or limited
- Dust, gases, and fumes at the worksite must be controlled through ventilation or protective equipment
- Workers should never be assigned tasks beyond their physical capacity or training without supervision

## Trade-Specific Safety Notes

### Construction / Masonry
- Wear a helmet and safety boots at all times on site
- Never work on scaffolding without a secured harness above ground-floor level
- Check that ladders and scaffolding are stable before climbing

### Electrical Work
- Always de-energize circuits before working on them where possible
- Use insulated tools and wear rubber-soled footwear
- Never work on live wiring during rain or in wet conditions

### Agricultural Labour
- Wear protective gloves and masks when handling pesticides or fertilizers
- Follow safe operating procedure around tractors and farm machinery
- Stay hydrated and take rest breaks during peak heat hours

## Reporting Unsafe Conditions
Workers have the right to report unsafe working conditions. Under the BOCW Rules, sites should have a designated Safety Officer or Safety Committee (for larger establishments) responsible for addressing safety complaints. LabourConnect's "Report Suspicious Job" feature can also be used to flag employers/sites with clear safety violations.

## Accident Response
In case of a workplace accident:
1. Provide immediate first aid
2. Report the accident to the site supervisor and, where serious, to the Labour Inspector
3. The injured worker (if registered with KBOCWWB) may be eligible for accident compensation or disability pension — see the schemes document for details
