# MGNREGA - Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act

Source: nrega.nic.in (official portal), Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India

## What It Is
MGNREGA (also called NREGA) is a central law passed in 2005 that guarantees rural households the "right to work." Any adult member of a rural household willing to do unskilled manual work is entitled to at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment per financial year.

## Key Guarantees
- **100 days per household per year** (not per person) — some drought-affected or special areas may get more
- **Work within 5 km** of the applicant's residence, wherever possible
- **Minimum wage** must be paid for the work, at the state-notified NREGA wage rate
- **15-day guarantee**: if work isn't provided within 15 days of applying, the applicant is legally entitled to an **unemployment allowance** as compensation
- **15-day payment rule**: wages must be paid within 15 days of the work being completed; delayed payment can require compensation to the worker
- Equal wages for men and women for the same work

## What Kind of Work Qualifies
Mostly unskilled manual labour on public works that create durable rural assets, such as:
- Rural roads
- Water conservation and irrigation works (ponds, canals, wells)
- Land development
- Drought-proofing and flood-control works

## How to Apply for a Job Card
1. Visit the local Gram Panchayat (village council) office
2. Submit an application (Form 1) with proof of address (Aadhaar, voter ID)
3. The Gram Panchayat must issue a **Job Card** within 15 days
4. The Job Card lists all adult household members eligible to demand work, and is used to track attendance and wage payments
5. To actually get work, the worker (or household) submits a written demand for work at the Gram Panchayat

## Karnataka Wage Rate
The MGNREGA daily wage rate is revised annually and varies by state — Karnataka's rate is notified separately each financial year by the central government. Because this changes yearly, **do not quote a fixed rupee figure without checking the latest official notification** at nrega.nic.in.

## Worker Rights
- All MGNREGA records (muster rolls, wage payment registers, work proposals) are public information and can be requested via RTI (Right to Information Act)
- Social audits of MGNREGA work must be conducted twice a year at the Gram Panchayat level, and audit findings are public
- Workers can check their job card status, attendance, and wage payments online at nrega.nic.in under Reports

## Important Note — Recent Changes to Verify
As of mid-2026, there are reports of a significant restructuring of the MGNREGA framework (renamed in some sources to a new central scheme with a 125-day guarantee instead of 100, plus a seasonal pause during peak farming months). This information comes from non-official websites and could not be fully confirmed from the official government portal at the time this document was written. **Before quoting exact day-guarantees or wage figures to users, verify directly against the current notification at nrega.nic.in** — the 100-day / job card process described above is the long-standing, well-established version of the scheme and remains a safe baseline for general guidance.
