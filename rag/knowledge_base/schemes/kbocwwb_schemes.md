# Karnataka Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (KBOCWWB) - Schemes

Source: karbwwb.karnataka.gov.in (official Karnataka government portal)

## Overview
The KBOCWWB was set up under the Building and Other Construction Workers Act, 1996 to provide social security and welfare benefits to registered construction workers in Karnataka. Workers must register with the Board and hold a valid beneficiary ID card to access any scheme below.

## Medical / Hospitalisation Assistance
Registered workers (and their dependents) hospitalised for a minimum of 48 continuous hours in a government hospital, or a private hospital recognised under state insurance schemes, can claim financial assistance of ₹300 per day of hospitalisation, up to a maximum of ₹20,000 per hospitalisation period. The application must be submitted within 6 months of the hospitalisation start date, along with the worker's ID card, hospital bills, and treatment records. Applications are verified by a Labour Inspector before approval.

## Educational Assistance
Financial help is available for the education of a registered worker's children, limited to two children per worker. It only covers regular, in-person enrolment at institutions physically located in Karnataka — distance education, home study, and online courses do not qualify.

## Pension
Registered workers become eligible for pension benefits after reaching the qualifying age. The pension amount is capped by government notification (subject to periodic revision) and is paid directly into the beneficiary's bank account. If the Board determines an applicant is ineligible, the applicant is entitled to a hearing before rejection.

## Other Benefits
- **Marriage assistance** for registered workers or their children
- **Thayi Magu Sahaya Hastha scheme** — maternity/childcare support for registered women construction workers
- **LPG connection assistance** (Karmika Bhagya scheme)
- **Concessional bus passes** — both for registered workers (in Bengaluru Metropolitan area) and student bus passes for their children on KSRTC
- **Accident compensation** and **disability pension** for workers partially or fully disabled due to a workplace accident or disease
- **Housing assistance** — advances toward government housing schemes, recovered from the worker in equal annual instalments over an extended period

## How to Apply
1. Worker submits application with required documents (ID card, employment certificate, bank account proof)
2. Verification by Labour Inspector / Senior Labour Inspector
3. Review and approval by the Labour Officer or Board Secretary
4. Approved amount disbursed directly to the worker's bank account

## Registration Eligibility
- Must be a construction worker aged 18-60
- Must have worked at least 90 days in building/construction work in the preceding 12 months
- Must register with the Board and obtain a beneficiary ID/smart card

*Note: Exact rupee amounts for pensions and other caps are revised periodically by government notification — verify current figures against the latest notification before quoting exact numbers to users.*
