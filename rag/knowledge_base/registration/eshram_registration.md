# e-Shram Card - National Database of Unorganised Workers

Source: eshram.gov.in (official portal), Ministry of Labour & Employment, Government of India

## What It Is
e-Shram is a national registration portal, launched in August 2021, that creates a central government database of all unorganised sector workers in India — covering construction workers, farm labour, domestic workers, street vendors, gig/platform workers, and similar categories. Registration gives each worker a permanent identity for accessing social security benefits.

## Who Can Register
- Age 16 to 59
- Indian resident
- Working in the unorganised sector (i.e. **not** already covered by EPFO, ESIC, or NPS — those are formal-sector schemes and are mutually exclusive with e-Shram)
- Aadhaar number with a linked, active mobile number is required for registration (OTP verification)

## What You Get
- A **Universal Account Number (UAN)** — a permanent 12-digit ID, valid for life and valid anywhere in India (very useful for migrant workers moving between states, since benefits and identity carry over)
- **Accident insurance** coverage — commonly cited as up to ₹2 lakh for accidental death or permanent total disability, and a lower amount for partial disability, under the PM Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) linkage
- Automatic eligibility flagging for other welfare schemes (state welfare boards, PM-SYM pension, Ayushman Bharat where applicable)
- A digital card that can be downloaded and re-downloaded anytime using your mobile number or UAN

## How to Register
1. Visit eshram.gov.in and select "Register on e-Shram"
2. Enter Aadhaar number and Aadhaar-linked mobile number, verify via OTP
3. Confirm auto-filled Aadhaar details
4. Enter bank account number and IFSC code (for direct benefit transfer)
5. Fill in occupation category (30+ options — construction worker, agricultural labour, domestic worker, driver, etc.), education, and estimated monthly income
6. Submit — the UAN and digital card are generated instantly

**Registration is completely free** on the official portal. It's also available in person at any Common Service Centre (CSC), typically for a small service fee (₹20-30) if you don't have internet access or need help with the process.

## Common Registration Problems
- **"Already registered" error** — Aadhaar is de-duplicated in the system; the worker should use "Already Registered" → retrieve UAN via mobile OTP, rather than registering again
- **OTP not arriving** — usually means the mobile number isn't correctly linked to Aadhaar; needs to be fixed at a UIDAI Aadhaar Seva Kendra first
- **Registration blocked** — happens if the worker is already an EPFO/ESIC/NPS member (formal sector); e-Shram is specifically for unorganised workers only

## e-Shram vs. State Welfare Boards (like KBOCWWB)
These are **not the same thing** and are not mutually exclusive — a construction worker can and should register for both:
- **e-Shram** = national identity + database + basic accident insurance, useful across any state (especially for migrant workers)
- **KBOCWWB** = Karnataka-specific construction worker welfare board with its own richer scheme benefits (medical, education, pension) — see the schemes document for details

Registering for e-Shram does not replace the need to also register with KBOCWWB if the worker specifically does construction work in Karnataka.
