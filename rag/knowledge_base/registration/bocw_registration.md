# Construction Worker Registration Process (KBOCWWB)

## Who Can Register
- Age 18-60
- Worked at least 90 days in building/construction work during the preceding 12 months
- Engaged in construction-related work (masonry, carpentry, painting, electrical, plumbing, etc.)

## Documents Required
- Proof of age (Aadhaar card, voter ID, or birth certificate)
- Proof of 90 days of construction work (employer certificate, or affidavit if unavailable)
- Passport-size photographs
- Bank account details (for benefit disbursal)
- Address proof

## Registration Steps
1. Worker submits Form V (application for registration) along with a certificate of employment and supporting documents to the Registering Officer
2. The Registering Officer verifies the application and supporting documents
3. Upon verification, the worker is issued a beneficiary Identity Card / smart card
4. The worker must renew registration periodically (typically annually) by paying a subscription fee — payable quarterly, half-yearly, or annually, in cash through Karnataka One (K1) centres or via employer/NGO bulk payment
5. Once registered, the worker becomes eligible to apply for any KBOCWWB welfare scheme

## e-Shram Registration (Central Government, all unorganised workers)
Separate from KBOCWWB, the e-Shram portal registers all unorganised sector workers nationally (not just construction) and issues a Universal Account Number (UAN). This enables portability of benefits — useful for migrant labourers who may work across state lines, since Karnataka-specific BOCW registration alone does not transfer to other states.

## Common Registration Problems (for Chatbot agent to help with)
- Worker lacks proof of 90 days of work — can use employer affidavit or union/NGO certification instead
- Worker is a migrant labourer without a local address — can still register using e-Shram as an alternative/supplement
- Bank account not linked — benefits cannot be disbursed until a valid bank account is on file
- ID card lost or damaged — worker can apply for a duplicate through the Registering Officer
